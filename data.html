Payment Composition

Cash (48.2%) and BCA QR (45.6%) together covered 93.8% of volume, so on-site visits drive the outlet.

Nearly half of customers already pay cashless, a sign of a modern, adaptive base. GoFood and GrabFood stay minimal, so delivery is still untapped.

Product Performance

Kare Ramen is the hero product at over Rp 23M, well ahead of the field.

SUMO Spicy Ramen follows near Rp 12M, with Rice Bowl steady at Rp 5 to 8M. The wide gap to second place points to reliance on one signature menu.

Strategic Roadmap

01
Lift average order valueBundle the hero product into combos and train staff to upsell higher-margin drinks, targeting AOV of Rp 28,000 to Rp 30,000.
02
Expand sales channelsRun exclusive promos on GoFood and GrabFood to capture the delivery demand that current cash and QR traffic leaves behind.
03
Protect the core and retainPrioritize Kare Ramen in stock planning to avoid lost sales, and use the high QR share to build a simple returning-customer program.
